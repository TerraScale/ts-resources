# Preserve data access at free-tier limits

The free tier does not expire and TerraScale does not delete or lock away customer data merely because the customer declines a paid plan or reaches a free allowance. At a limit, TerraScale preserves reads, exports, and deletion while rejecting new billable writes or resource creation with a clear upgrade response. A failed renewal receives a seven-day payment grace period with Polar retries and customer notices, then downgrades to Developer if unresolved. When a paid organization cancels within Developer limits, it downgrades at the end of the billing period. If it exceeds those limits, it enters a 30-day read-only grace period for export or usage reduction before its organization data is deleted under the accepted retention policy. Automatic overage charges remain disabled until provider-attributed metering, reconciliation, customer-visible usage, disputes, refunds, and failure handling pass production checks.
