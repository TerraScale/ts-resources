# Use four customer plans

TerraScale uses Developer, Starter, Pro, and Enterprise plans. Developer is the default, non-expiring free plan and creates no paid subscription or charge. Every User receives one non-transferable Personal Organization, and every account enrolls a verified card for abuse prevention, including Developer. Payment methods, Polar Customer identity, subscriptions, trials, and billing history belong to the Organization rather than the User. Each verified User may own one Developer Organization; additional owned Organizations require Starter or higher, while membership in Organizations owned by other Users does not consume the allowance. Reusing a card is an abuse signal, not an automatic rejection. Starter targets $5 USD per month and Pro targets $25 USD per month. An eligible paid Organization receives one 30-day trial only when its owner explicitly selects the paid plan and accepts renewal. Trial eligibility survives card, owner, and membership changes and cannot be reset by changing those identities. Initial entitlements are:

| Plan | Projects | Databases | Members | Storage across regions | Monthly operation units |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Developer | 1 | 3 | 3 | 1 GB | 10,000 |
| Starter | 5 | 10 | 5 | 5 GB | 1,000,000 |
| Pro | 25 | 50 | 25 | 50 GB | 10,000,000 |

All three plans may use the launch regions and create any number of environments within their shared allowances. Enterprise has a $500 USD monthly support base and no included usage, so all measured storage and operation usage is billed separately. Public Alpha supports monthly billing only. Enterprise and annual billing remain unavailable until their usage, support, cancellation, refund, tax, and discount behavior pass production checks. The previous Scale and Business plans are removed.
