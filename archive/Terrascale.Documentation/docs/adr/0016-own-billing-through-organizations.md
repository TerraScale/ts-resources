# Own billing through Organizations

Every User receives one non-transferable Personal Organization. Payment methods, Polar Customer identity, subscriptions, trials, and billing history attach only to an Organization, never directly to a User. The Personal Organization may be renamed, contain Projects, and use the selected plan's ordinary member allowance. Every account enrolls a verified card for abuse prevention, including Developer, while Developer creates no subscription or charge. Each User may own one Developer Organization, and additional owned Organizations require Starter or Pro. Membership in another User's Organization does not consume this allowance. Reused cards contribute to abuse evaluation but do not automatically block registration. Trial eligibility is durable across card, owner, and membership changes.
