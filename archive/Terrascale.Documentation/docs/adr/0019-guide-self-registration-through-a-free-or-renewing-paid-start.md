# Guide self-registration through a free or renewing paid start

The target Customer onboarding contract opens with identity and email verification already counted as the completed first step. It then guides a self-registering User through provisioning the first Organization, Project, and logical Database and enrolling a payment card for signup abuse control. Card verification creates no charge, paid trial, subscription, or automatic renewal. After verification, the User explicitly chooses either no paid plan and no automatic renewal, retaining the non-expiring Developer allowance, or Starter or Pro with monthly automatic renewal. An eligible paid Organization receives its first 30 days at no charge. Invited-user onboarding remains a separate flow and does not require signup card verification or a plan choice. This decision refines the onboarding sequence and paid-trial trigger in ADR 0004 and ADR 0016. The target is not fully released; implementation is tracked in [TerraScale.Internals issue 30](https://git.terrascale.tech/TerraScale/TerraScale.Internals/issues/30) and [TerraScale.TypeScript issue 6](https://git.terrascale.tech/TerraScale/TerraScale.TypeScript/issues/6).
