# Notify affected Organization Owners after confirmation

TerraScale emails the Organization Owners of each affected organization after confirming that a security incident affected them. The initial notice may state that scope and root cause remain under investigation, and TerraScale follows applicable legal deadlines rather than waiting for every detail to become final. The notice identifies the known affected period and data categories, current mitigations, and any customer action. Customer organizations notify their own application users when their role requires it.
