This policy applies to accounts, API clients, agents, and Customer content used with TerraScale.

## Do not misuse TerraScale

Customers must not use TerraScale to:

- break the law or violate another person's rights;
- gain access to an account, Project, Database, Environment, Repository, Document, system, or network without permission;
- distribute malware, steal credentials, conduct phishing, or operate infrastructure for fraud;
- evade plan allowances, rate limits, authentication, authorization, abuse controls, or suspension;
- disrupt TerraScale or another customer's use through denial-of-service traffic, destructive automation, or deliberate resource exhaustion;
- probe or exploit a vulnerability outside the coordinated disclosure policy;
- send spam or use the service to coordinate harassment, threats, exploitation, or violence;
- store or process credentials, cryptographic keys, payment-card data, health records, government identifiers, children's data, unlawful content, or workloads subject to special regulation during Public Alpha;
- use Customer content or service output to violate privacy, intellectual property, employment, consumer, export-control, or sanctions law.

Ordinary lawful application content is allowed. TerraScale does not prohibit content merely because it is commercial, user-generated, encrypted by the customer before upload, or unpopular. The Public Alpha data restrictions above exist because the matching legal and operational controls are not yet qualified.

## Enforcement

TerraScale may rate-limit, isolate, or suspend activity while investigating a credible risk. It may remove unlawful data when required by law. When safe and lawful, TerraScale gives the Organization Owner notice and a way to explain or correct the issue.

Severe or repeated violations may result in termination. Customers retain export and deletion rights unless preserving evidence, protecting people, or complying with law requires a narrower temporary restriction.
