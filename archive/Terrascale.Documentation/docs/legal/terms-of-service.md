## Service

TerraScale Public Alpha is an early release for testing ordinary application data. It is not approved for critical data or workloads subject to special regulation. Public Alpha has no uptime percentage or service-level agreement. Features, limits, and interfaces may change under the versioning and notice terms published with the service.

Customers must keep account credentials secure, assign the minimum required access, and ensure their use and Customer content comply with law and the Acceptable Use Policy.

## Accounts and authority

The user must provide accurate registration information, verify the account and payment method, and be authorized to accept these Terms for the relevant organization. Organization Owners control membership, subscriptions, export, and deletion requests.

TerraScale records the accepted policy versions, user, organization when applicable, time, and request evidence. Registration cannot complete unless the user affirmatively accepts every active required policy.

## Plans and monthly billing

Developer is a non-expiring $0 plan. A verified user may own one Developer organization. A self-registering user must enroll and verify a payment card for signup abuse control, but verification creates no subscription, charge, paid trial, or automatic renewal. After verification, the user may choose no paid plan and no automatic renewal while retaining the Developer allowance.

Starter costs $5 USD monthly and Pro costs $25 USD monthly. An eligible paid organization receives its first 30 days at no charge only after an Organization Owner explicitly selects Starter or Pro and accepts monthly automatic renewal. Eligibility comes from durable billing history and is not restored by changing the card, owner, or membership. Enterprise is unavailable during Public Alpha.

TerraScale uses hard storage and operation-unit allowances. It rejects new billable work at an allowance while preserving reads, project export, deletion, and usage reduction. Public Alpha does not bill egress or usage above those allowances.

## Renewal, cancellation, and payment failure

Starter and Pro renew monthly until an Organization Owner cancels or changes the plan. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period. An organization above Developer limits enters a 30-day read-only grace period after cancellation or downgrade.

An unresolved renewal payment receives a seven-day payment grace period. If it remains unresolved, the organization enters the same 30-day read-only grace period. During that period, customers may read, export, delete, and reduce usage. New billable work stays blocked.

## Export and deletion

Customers may export each Project as a compressed package with a manifest and one NDJSON file per Repository. Credential secrets are excluded. Export remains available during the 30-day grace period.

Account deletion requires recent authentication and explicit confirmation. It starts a seven-day cancellation window, revokes sessions, and stops new charges. After that window TerraScale removes memberships and Customer content, retains only records required for billing, security, or law, and produces a deletion receipt.

## Support and incidents

Developer receives documentation and best-effort email support. Starter targets a response within two business days. Pro targets a response within one business day. These targets are not resolution guarantees.

Planned maintenance receives at least 24 hours notice. Emergency maintenance may begin without advance notice. Public incident communication follows the published status and incident policy.

## Suspension

TerraScale may limit or suspend access to protect the service, other customers, or the public; address suspected fraud or security compromise; enforce hard allowances; comply with law; or address a material breach. When safe and lawful, TerraScale will state the reason and the steps available to restore access. Suspension does not erase export and deletion rights except where law or an active security response requires a narrower temporary restriction.
