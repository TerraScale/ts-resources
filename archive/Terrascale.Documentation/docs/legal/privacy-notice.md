## Data categories

**Customer content** is the Document data and related Repository data that customers submit. TerraScale stores Customer content only in the regions selected for the Database, subject to temporary movement required to add, drain, repair, export, or delete an actual selected-region placement.

**Control metadata** is the minimum information needed to operate accounts and the service. It includes account and organization identifiers, membership and authorization records, selected-region configuration, resource identifiers and states, subscription and invoice references, payment-provider identifiers, security events, audit events, service monitoring, support correspondence, and deletion receipts. TerraScale may process Control metadata in its control regions even when those regions are not selected for Customer content.

TerraScale does not ask customers to place credentials, cryptographic keys, payment-card data, health records, government identifiers, children's data, unlawful content, or specially regulated workloads in Customer content during Public Alpha.

## Purposes and lawful bases

TerraScale processes account and Control metadata to provide and secure the service, administer subscriptions, prevent abuse, measure allowances, respond to support, communicate incidents, maintain audit history, comply with legal duties, and establish or defend legal claims. The lawful basis for each purpose requires Brazilian legal review: LEGAL_TBD_LAWFUL_BASES.

Payment-card details are handled by the payment provider. TerraScale retains provider references and billing records needed to administer the subscription and comply with law.

## Sharing and transfers

TerraScale shares data only with authorized customer users, service providers needed to operate TerraScale, professional advisers, or public authorities when law requires it. The subprocessors, transfer mechanisms, and locations require final review: LEGAL_TBD_SUBPROCESSORS_AND_TRANSFERS.

## Retention

Customer content follows the active subscription, grace-period, export, and deletion schedule. Account deletion has a seven-day cancellation window. After deletion, TerraScale keeps only billing, security, audit, and legally required records for the periods applicable to each record type. This draft does not invent exact legal retention periods. The approved schedule must come from counsel and operational evidence: LEGAL_TBD_RETENTION_SCHEDULE.

Monitoring history is retained for 7 days on Developer, 30 days on Starter, and 90 days on Pro. Billing records follow their separate legal schedule.

## Rights and requests

People may request confirmation, access, correction, portability where applicable, deletion, information about sharing, and review of relevant automated decisions under applicable law. Identity verification, request channel, response process, and legal exceptions require approval: LEGAL_TBD_DATA_SUBJECT_PROCESS.

## Security and incidents

Customer traffic requires TLS 1.2 or newer. Data at rest uses qualified storage and key-management controls. Encryption does not replace authorization. TerraScale records security, monitoring, and audit data needed to detect and investigate misuse. Confirmed security incidents are communicated to affected Organization Owners and authorities as applicable.

## Changes

Material changes receive a new policy version. TerraScale requests renewed acceptance when law or the change requires it.
