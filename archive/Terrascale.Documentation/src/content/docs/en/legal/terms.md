## Plans and billing

Developer is a non-expiring $0 plan. Self-registration requires payment-card verification for signup abuse control, but verification creates no paid subscription, charge, paid trial, or automatic renewal. After verification, a User may choose no paid plan and no automatic renewal while retaining the Developer allowance. Starter is $5 USD monthly and Pro is $25 USD monthly. An eligible paid Organization receives its first 30 days at no charge only after an Organization Owner explicitly selects Starter or Pro and accepts monthly automatic renewal. Eligibility comes from durable billing history and is not restored by changing the card, owner, or membership. Enterprise is unavailable during Public Alpha.

Developer, Starter, and Pro use hard storage and operation-unit allowances. Reaching an allowance blocks new billable work while preserving reads, project export, deletion, and usage reduction. Public Alpha does not bill egress or paid overages.

An unresolved renewal payment receives seven days of payment grace, then a 30-day read-only grace period. Cancellation or downgrade that leaves an organization above Developer limits uses the same 30-day state. During it, customers can read, export, delete, and reduce usage. Account deletion has a seven-day cancellation window before Customer content is removed and a deletion receipt is produced. Records required by billing, security, audit, or law may be retained under the approved schedule.

## Conduct, support, and acceptance

Customers must follow the [Acceptable Use Policy](/legal/acceptable-use/), keep credentials secure, and use only data they are authorized to process. Developer receives best-effort email support, Starter targets two business days, and Pro targets one business day. These are response targets, not resolution or uptime promises.

Registration must record affirmative acceptance of every active required policy version, the user, organization where relevant, time, and request evidence before payment verification completes. See the [Privacy Notice](/legal/privacy/) and [Data Processing Terms](/legal/data-processing-terms/).
