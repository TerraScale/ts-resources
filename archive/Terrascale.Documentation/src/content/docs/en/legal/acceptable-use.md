**Status: draft. Owner approval: pending.** This proposed policy is for review before registration and remains subject to the unresolved company and legal fields marked `LEGAL_TBD`.

Customers must not use TerraScale to break the law or another person's rights, access an account or resource without permission, distribute malware or steal credentials, evade allowances or abuse controls, disrupt the service or another customer, probe a vulnerability outside the coordinated disclosure policy, send spam, or coordinate harassment, threats, exploitation, or violence.

Customers must not submit credentials, cryptographic keys, payment-card data, health records, government identifiers, children's data, unlawful content, or specially regulated workloads during Public Alpha. Ordinary lawful database content is allowed. Content is not restricted merely because it is commercial, user-generated, encrypted by the customer, or unpopular.

TerraScale may rate-limit, isolate, or suspend credible risk while investigating. When safe and lawful, it gives the Organization Owner notice and a way to explain or correct the issue. Report abuse to `LEGAL_TBD_ABUSE_EMAIL` and suspected vulnerabilities to [security@terrascale.tech](mailto:security@terrascale.tech).
