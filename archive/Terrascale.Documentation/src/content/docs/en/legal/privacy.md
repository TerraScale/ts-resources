## Data we handle

Customer content is Document and Repository data stored in the Database's selected regions. Temporary movement may occur while a selected placement catches up, drains, repairs, exports, or deletes. Minimal Control metadata, including account and organization identifiers, membership and authorization records, selected-region configuration, resource state, billing references, usage, monitoring, audit, security, support, and deletion receipts, may be processed in TerraScale control regions.

TerraScale does not ask customers to submit credentials, cryptographic keys, payment-card data, health records, government identifiers, children's data, unlawful content, or specially regulated workloads during Public Alpha.

## Purposes and rights

TerraScale uses account and Control metadata to provide and secure the service, administer billing and allowances, prevent abuse, answer support, communicate incidents, maintain audit history, comply with legal duties, and establish or defend claims. Payment-card details are handled by the payment provider; TerraScale keeps provider references and billing records needed for administration and law.

People may request applicable confirmation, access, correction, portability, deletion, sharing information, and review of automated decisions. Identity checks, response channels, exceptions, and exact retention periods require legal approval and remain `LEGAL_TBD`.

## Security and changes

Customer traffic requires TLS 1.2 or newer. Data at rest uses qualified storage and key-management controls. Encryption does not replace authorization. Confirmed security incidents are communicated to affected Organization Owners. Material changes receive a new policy version and renewed acceptance when required.
