Email [support@terrascale.tech](mailto:support@terrascale.tech) with account, billing, or technical questions. Include the affected project, approximate time, public error code, and a request ID when one is available. Do not send credentials or Customer content.

## Response targets

| Plan | Support during Public Alpha |
| --- | --- |
| Developer | Documentation and best-effort email support |
| Starter | Email support with a target of two business days |
| Pro | Priority email support with a target of one business day |
| Enterprise | Unavailable. Enterprise support hours, severity levels, escalation, and service credits are not defined. |

These are response targets, not resolution promises or an uptime SLA. For shared service incidents, check [service status and incident communication](/about/status-and-incidents/).

Report suspected security vulnerabilities through the [coordinated disclosure policy](/security/), not through ordinary support.
